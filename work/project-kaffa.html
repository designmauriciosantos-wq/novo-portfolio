A complete identity, built around one letterform.

Kaffa is a coffee, brunch & wine bar on the Douro Marina in Afurada, Vila Nova de Gaia. The identity covers the full system — logo, colour, typography, pattern — carried through every real touchpoint: signage, social, tableware, loyalty, merchandise, and print.

// WHY THIS MATTERS FOR DESIGN WORK

This project is the clearest proof I have of a full identity system held together end to end — a typographic and colour system carried through pattern, tableware, loyalty, merchandise, social and print, verified against real photography rather than mockups alone. It's the same discipline I bring to any brand: make one small detail — a ligature, a colour value — do structural work across everything the brand touches.
