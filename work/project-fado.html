Making a month-old wine sound like it's always been here.

Developed during my postgraduate degree in Creative Communication and Advertising at Universidade Católica de Lisboa. The brief: Mandriola de Lisboa — a wine brand barely a month old, named after the city's calm, carefree street cats — needed a campaign that could carry it through its first three years.

View on Behance →
FADO MANDRIOLA — CAMPAIGN COVER

A brand this new has no history of its own — so we borrowed one, on purpose.

Fado is the musical style tourists already associate with "real" Lisbon — melancholic, traditional, instantly recognisable. Rather than compete with that association, Fado Mandriola leaned into it: pairing the brand's cat mascot with real Fado imagery and figures, so a brand-new wine could borrow the emotional weight of a century-old tradition, while still feeling playful rather than reverent.

// OUT OF HOME

Banners and street presence

Proposed placements across the city — bus shelters, posters, banners — putting the campaign where tourists already look for "authentic" Lisbon.

// THE JINGLE

An original Fado, written for the brand

Rather than license an existing song, the campaign includes an original Fado composed specifically for Mandriola — "Mandriolings" — carrying the brand's name into the tradition itself.

// PRINT — ON THE BOTTLE

Fado icons, worn like jewellery

"Gargantilhas" — bottle neck tags styled as Fado singers' necklaces — put specific, recognisable figures directly on the product itself.
