Jantar Vínico — four dinners, always sold out.

Jantar Vínico is a recurring wine-pairing dinner series at Armazém do Peixe — each edition built around a different producer, 25 guests a night. Rather than a single event, this became a full communication campaign: a landing page to anchor each edition, paired with social videos built to pull bookings straight from Instagram. Along the way, it also became the launchpad for the restaurant's new premium space, the Sala Vínica.
